\section{Interpretation}
Scanning primarily targets a handful of ports such as 8124, 6066 and 5060, suggesting automation scripts aimed at common services. The time series shows nearly steady traffic with minor fluctuations, indicating mass scanning rather than short bursts. The heavy hitter probes hundreds of addresses, reinforcing the pattern of broad reconnaissance.

Selecting the heavy hitter scanner, its steady activity against few ports hints at an automated mass scanner rather than a targeted probe. Such hosts generally pose minimal direct risk if the telescope is non-routable, yet the underlying intent is malicious reconnaissance and the IP may belong to a larger botnet.
